What Awaits Inside

Every exhibit is generated from Wikipedia — no two visits are the same.

Infinite Exhibits

Any Wikipedia article becomes an explorable exhibit. Halls connect based on real article links — follow your curiosity endlessly.

Multiplayer

Explore together. Host a lobby, invite friends, and navigate the museum in real-time with chat, mounts, and shared discoveries.

Wiki Races

Compete to reach a target article first. Environmental events — fog, earthquakes, reverse controls — keep every race unpredictable.

Tournaments

Bracket-style tournaments with live standings. Per-round results, champion crowns, and the glory of knowledge.

52 Environmental Events

Low gravity, silence, color shifts, weather, darkness — random events transform the museum during races for chaotic fun.

13 Languages

Fully localized interface and lobby content in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese, Bengali, Italian, Korean, Dutch, Polish, and Russian.
